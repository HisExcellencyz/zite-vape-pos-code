# Uptown Vapes — POS & Inventory Tracker (KES)

A complete point-of-sale and inventory management system for **Uptown Vapes**, a vape retail business operating in Kenya (KES currency).

## Who it's for
Staff and managers at Uptown Vapes branches — used for daily sales, inventory tracking, supplier management, and business reporting.

## What it does
- **POS**: Ring up sales with product search, cart, customer linking, payment methods, and delivery tracking (pickup points, stops, distance)
- **Inventory**: Full product catalog with categories, stock levels, import/export, and bulk operations
- **Purchase Orders (LPO)**: Create and manage local purchase orders, download as PDF or Excel to share with suppliers, verify deliveries, and auto-record as purchases
- **Suppliers & Deposits**: Track supplier relationships, deposit balances, and transactions
- **Customers**: Customer database with location/coordinates for delivery tracking
- **Addresses**: Manage commonly used pickup points and delivery addresses with Google Maps integration
- **Expenses**: Track other business expenses by category
- **Reports**: Dashboard with KPIs, revenue trends, expense breakdowns, top products/customers, and delivery distance stats (longest, shortest, average)
- **User Management**: Invite users, assign roles with granular permissions
- **Multi-branch**: Branch-aware data across all features
